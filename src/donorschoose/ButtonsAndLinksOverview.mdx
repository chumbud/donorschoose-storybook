# Buttons and Links

Two parts, and the choice between them is about **what happens on click**, not
about emphasis: a button performs an action, a link goes somewhere. Reach for a
[Button](?path=/docs/components-buttons-and-links-button--docs) when the click
changes something — adding to a cart, submitting, opening a dialog — and a
[Link](?path=/story/components-buttons-and-links-links--links) when it navigates.
A quiet action is still a button, and a prominent navigation is still a link.

## The parts

{/* Links have to be markdown: MDX maps those to Storybook's anchor, which
    navigates the manager. A raw <a> renders as a plain anchor and resolves
    `?path=` against the preview iframe, which loads nothing. */}

- **[Button](?path=/docs/components-buttons-and-links-button--docs)** —
  **primary** (filled blue CTA) and **secondary** (outlined), each with the states
  used across the site: hover, focus, active, loading, disabled, small,
  full-width, plus the destructive and overlay tones.
- **[Links](?path=/story/components-buttons-and-links-links--links)** — the four
  link treatments: the default `<a>`, `.text-link`, `.link-discreet`, and
  `.subtle-link`.

## Which link treatment?

- **Default `<a>`** — blue, no underline at rest, underline plus a slight darken
  on hover. The everyday link.
- **`.text-link`** — a `<button>` (or `<a>`) that reads as an inline blue link,
  with no border or background. For inline actions inside a sentence: “Learn
  more”, “Cancel”.
- **`.link-discreet`** — muted grey with a dotted, half-opacity underline that
  turns solid black on hover. For quiet, low-emphasis links.
- **`.subtle-link`** — near-black with a dotted underline that turns blue on
  hover.

## Notes

- Both parts are ported from `donorschoose-web`: buttons from
  `base/form-elements/_buttons.scss`, links from `general.scss` and the same
  buttons file.
- `fullWidth` buttons stretch to their container but cap at **550px** and center,
  matching the site.
- `warning` and `overlay` are secondary-only tones; setting either on a primary
  button resolves to secondary so the tone actually shows.
- The real class is **`link-discreet`**, not `discreet-link` — the latter doesn't
  exist in the codebase.
